# My project brief

## The question
How does traffic congestion on one route in Lagos mainland  affect other routes?

## Why it matters.
It will help us understand why and how traffic spreads.

## Data needed and sources
- LGA boundary - Grid 3 https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-operational-lga-boundaries/about
- Road  - Geofabrik  https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-operational-lga-boundaries/about
- Travel speed - Google Earth pro

## What would i build
A webapp or dashboard that shows the distribution of traffic along a route and its relationship with other route. it can also predict the next possible routes that is most likely to be affected if traffic persists.
